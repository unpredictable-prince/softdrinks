# Fizzora - Staff & Administrator User Guide

## Introduction
Fizzora Business Management Hub empowers soft drink brand operators and field executives in Gujarat to manage operations seamlessly without disconnected spreadsheets.

---

## 1. Public Portal & Customer Journey
- **Brand Story & Flavour Exploration**: Visitors discover beverage varieties (Jeera Masala, Lemon Fizz, Kachi Keri, Cola Fizz, etc.).
- **Retailer Partnerships**: Local shopkeepers, distributors, and banquet halls submit wholesale demand via the B2B form or connect via direct WhatsApp chat.
- **Direct Calling & Socials**: Quick links connect to regional business lines and official social feeds.

---

## 2. Admin Hub Access
1. Navigate to `/admin/login`.
2. Enter your authorized business credentials.
3. Access role-specific panels:
   - **Admin/Owner**: Full visibility, product creation, sales approval, payment verification, stock adjustments, financial reports.
   - **Staff**: Enquiry follow-ups, draft sales creation, payment entry.

---

## 3. Managing Products & Bottle Variants
- Navigate to **Products**.
- Create or edit soft drink products with their descriptive flavour notes and imagery.
- Configure Variants per bottle format (e.g. 200ml glass/PET, 500ml PET, 1.25L family pack).
- Set Maximum Retail Price (MRP), distributor selling price, and unit cost price for gross margin analysis.
- Define `reorder_threshold` to trigger automatic low-stock alerts.

---

## 4. Converting Leads into Customers
1. Open **Enquiries** to review newly received B2B leads.
2. Update follow-up status (e.g., `CONTACTED` or `QUALIFIED`) with notes from phone/in-person meetings.
3. Click **Convert to Customer** to auto-populate a customer record in the directory.

---

## 5. Sales Recording & Invoicing
1. Navigate to **Sales** -> **New Sale**.
2. Select the customer/retailer, sales channel (e.g. Field Visit, WhatsApp, Phone), and add items.
3. Quantity, unit price, discounts, and taxes calculate automatically.
4. Saving as **Draft** leaves inventory untouched.
5. Moving status to **Confirmed** or **Fulfilled** verifies warehouse stock and deducts items in real-time.

---

## 6. Payments & Allocations
1. Soft drinks are often sold on partial credit to local kirana stores.
2. In **Payments**, record payments received (Cash, UPI with UTR reference, Bank Transfer).
3. Allocate received amounts to specific sales invoices.
4. The system updates the customer's outstanding balance automatically. Over-allocation is prevented.

---

## 7. Inventory Tracking & Warehouse Audits
- View **Inventory** for live stock counts across variants.
- Items below threshold are flagged with warning badges.
- Stock movements (receipts from bottling line, damaged bottles, manual adjustments) must be logged with a clear business reason.

---

## 8. Business Intelligence & Reports
- **Dashboard**: Track Gross Sales, Cash Received, Total Outstanding Balances, and Gross Profit.
- **Channel Analytics**: Evaluate which channels (WhatsApp, direct field sales, website enquiries) drive the highest volume.
- **CSV Exports**: One-click download of sales records and stock balances for tax and auditing purposes.
